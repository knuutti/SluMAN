    How to Use

    Making a skin

    1. Open a PNG holding the controller's body and every button and stick, released and pressed.
    2. Crop the base: the controller body everything is placed on.
    3. Pick a slot in the Parts list, then click its sprite on the sprite sheet to crop it, or drag a box around it.
    4. Drag the part on the layout to place it. Check it with Released / Pressed, or Live with your controller.
    5. Name the skin and save. It goes to your skins folder and shows in the Input Display and on the OBS page.

    Mouse and keyboard

    Click a spriteCrop the selected slot to it.
    Click a cropSelect its slot. Alt+click crops the sprite under it instead.
    Drag on the sheetDraw a crop; drag its handles to resize it.
    Arrow keysNudge 1 px in the pane used last; with Shift, 10 px.
    Mouse wheelZoom.
    Middle drag, Space+dragPan. Dragging empty layout pans too.
    Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+YUndo, redo.
    Ctrl+O, Ctrl+SOpen PNG, save.
