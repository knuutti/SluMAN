    How to

    Pick a slot on the left, then click its sprite on the sheet to crop it, or drag a box around it. Drag the part on the layout to place it. Click a crop on the sheet to select it; Alt+click crops the sprite under a crop.

    ←↑→↓ nudge 1 px, with Shift 10 px, in the pane you used last. Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Y redo. Scroll to zoom; drag with the middle button or hold Space to pan.

    Make an input display skin

    Start from one PNG holding the controller's body and every button and stick, released and pressed.

    1. Crop the base: the controller body everything is placed on.
    2. Crop each button's released and pressed look.
    3. Place them on the layout, check them with Released / Pressed or Live, and save.
    or drop or paste an image here, or open one of your skins at the top.
